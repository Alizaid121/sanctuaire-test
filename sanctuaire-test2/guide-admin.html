Test 2 · Guide for the English speaker (admin)

Test 2 checks the French text of the whole app. The French speaker reviews 258 short texts (buttons, titles, messages). You are the admin: you approve or reject her changes. Everything is saved and kept as history. At the end you export a PDF and send it to Claude in the chat.

Part 1 · One-time setup (you only, about 15 minutes)

Step 1 · Put the page on GitHub
  1. In the sanctuaire-test repository, create the folder sanctuaire-test2 (the same way sanctuaire-test1 is there).
  2. Upload the 5 files from the folder upload-to-github/sanctuaire-test2 of the package: index.html, strings.js, report.js, guide-admin.html, guide-fr.html.
  3. Do not delete or change sanctuaire-test1: the PDF report of Test 2 reuses its PDF files (vendor folder).
  4. Wait 1 to 2 minutes. The page will be at https://alizaid121.github.io/sanctuaire-test/sanctuaire-test2/
Never upload the two .sql files to GitHub. They are in the folder database of the package. The repository is public, and the second file will contain your PINs.
Step 2 · Create the Test 2 tables in Supabase
  1. Open your Supabase project (the same one as Test 1), then SQL Editor, then New query.
  2. Open 1-setup.sql, copy everything, paste it, press Run.
  3. You should see “Success. No rows returned”. It only adds new things whose names start with t2_. Test 1 is not touched.
Step 3 · Set the two PINs (the same as in Test 1)
  1. Open 2-set-pins.sql on your computer or phone and replace CHANGE-ME-ADMIN-PIN with your Test 1 PIN, and CHANGE-ME-PARTNER-PIN with the French speaker’s Test 1 PIN. Keep the quotes.
  2. Paste it in a new query in the SQL Editor and press Run. If you left a CHANGE-ME text, it stops with a message and changes nothing.
  3. Delete the edited file afterwards. The PINs are stored scrambled in the database; Test 1’s own PINs are not changed.

Test 2 keeps its own list of PINs, so you set them once more here. If you do not remember a Test 1 PIN, choose a new one and tell the French speaker.

Step 4 · Check it works
  1. Open the page. Press Check the connection. You should see “Connected. The Test 2 database is ready.”
  2. If it says the functions were not found, Step 2 was not run in the same Supabase project.
  3. Log in with your PIN. The page is in English. The French speaker’s PIN opens the same page in French.
Step 5 · Invite the French speaker

Send her the page link and the French guide: https://alizaid121.github.io/sanctuaire-test/sanctuaire-test2/guide-fr.html. She uses her Test 1 PIN.

Part 2 · How the test works

Each text shows: the English, the proposed French, where it appears in the app, and how much space it has on screen (a number of characters). Texts are grouped by category and each category shows how many are agreed.

StatusWhat happenedWho acts next
To reviewNobody has touched itFrench speaker
Agreed (original French)She approved the proposed French. Done.—
Change suggestedShe wrote another French (with an optional reason and note)You
Rejected, waiting for a replyYou rejected it with a noteFrench speaker
Reply sentShe answered your note, maybe with a new FrenchYou
Agreed (changed)You approved her version. Done.—
Your buttons
  • Approve her version: her French becomes the final one.
  • Reject with a note: the note is required. She sees it and can reply.
  • Keep the original: ends the discussion with Claude’s French. Use it if you cannot agree.
  • Reopen: on an agreed text, to discuss it again. The old history stays.

Use the filter Waiting for me to see only what needs you. Every step stays in the text’s History, and the page refreshes by itself every few seconds.

What to look at
  • Is it short enough? The page counts her letters against the space on screen.
  • Is it natural, in “tu”, and in the feminine where it speaks about her?
  • Parts in curly brackets like {name} or {n} are filled in by the app; she must keep them exactly (the page checks).

Part 3 · Finishing

  1. Work until nothing is left under Waiting for me, and the French speaker has nothing left to review. If you disagree for good, use Keep the original.
  2. Scroll to the bottom to Report (PDF). Choose English, press Download the PDF report.
  3. Send the PDF to Claude in the chat. It lists the summary, what is still open, the final French of every text, and the discussion history.
  4. Claude then builds the French screens (D9) from the final French.
Start again: at the very bottom, type RESET and press the button. It deletes every review and all history, and cannot be undone.

If something goes wrong

ProblemWhat to do
“Wrong PIN” / “Too many wrong tries”After 5 wrong tries the page pauses for 5 minutes. Wait, then try again. Check that Step 3 used the right PINs.
“Functions not found”Run 1-setup.sql in the same Supabase project as the page uses.
Page not found after uploadWait a few minutes. Check the folder name is exactly sanctuaire-test2 and that index.html is inside it.
The PDF cannot be createdThe folder sanctuaire-test1/vendor must still be in the repository.
Logged out by itselfSessions last about 11 hours. Log in again; nothing is lost.
You see an old versionPress Refresh on the page, or reload with the browser.

The 5 tries and 5 minutes pause are my choice, because I could not read Test 1’s database code. If Test 1 behaves differently, tell Claude.
